Inventory · three

Fields with no schema at all

Asked for by the finder, but there is nowhere to put them yet. Each needs a type change before it needs a source.

  • Coordinates — blocks everything geographic. First domino.
  • Surrounding amenities — shops, schools, clinics, food within walking range.
  • Facilities — gym, pool, visitor parking. Per development, so 29 not 301.
  • Floor, view, pets, move-in date, lease terms — per unit, per advert, and none of it in the search payload.
  • Photos, description text, agent contact — excluded by the publishing constraint, not by absence of a source.

Plan · ordered by leverage

Routes to the missing data

Numbered because the order matters: route 1 is a prerequisite for routes 2 and 3, and nothing else depends on anything.

01

Place every development on the map

done — 18 of 29

Solved on 19 Aug, and not by geocoding. Nominatim's freeform search managed 12 of 29 and put one development on a bus stop. What worked was querying Overpass for named buildings in a bounding box and matching locally — then rejecting any match landing outside the neighbourhood the listing already names. That gate caught four confident errors, including "Jaya Square" 11 km away for Metropolitan Square and the wrong Pinnacle for Pinnacle Kelana Jaya. Fourteen resolved to their own building; another fourteen to their street, since every record already carries an address.

02

Amenity counts — ready, needs a field

proven

One Overpass call returns 3,634 shops, schools, clinics, banks and eating places across the corridor, so counting what sits within 800 m of each development is arithmetic we can already do. Metropolitan Square: 25. Dataran Prima: 75. Armanee Terrace: 5. That is the "surrounding amenities" factor the scoring model asks for, and the only thing missing is somewhere in the schema to put it.

03

Phone the management office

only route left

Maintenance fees, lift counts and parking ratios are not published anywhere. Two full search passes — directories, then forums — produced 8 fees of 29, one lift count too ambiguous to use, and a single parking ratio. That well is dry. 29 phone calls replaces every uncorroborated fee with something real, and it is the highest-value hour on this list after route 1.

06

Furnishing — no viable route today

blocked

Not in the search payload, so it needs the detail page of all 301 adverts, from portals that return 403 and challenge by the third request. Saving 301 pages by hand is not a plan. This one waits for a licensing conversation, or stays "Not stated".

07

Reviews — not an acquisition problem

out of scope

The sources are other people's written reviews on PropSocial, iProperty and Google Maps. Summarising them into themes republishes their content, which is the same boundary the publish check enforces for listing descriptions. Needs to be first-party or licensed. Scraping is not the shortcut here; it is the thing already ruled out.

What no route should be built for

Photos, scraped description text and agent contact details are excluded by the project's one hard constraint, and it is enforced in code rather than trusted: the publish check fails on those key names, and the extractor drops them at collection. We do not hold them — descriptionText is populated in 0 of 29 raw files, and agent names and phone numbers are never stored at all. Acquiring them means overturning a settled decision and taking on PDPA exposure, not writing a scraper.
